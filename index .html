Frequently asked questions

Is StudyLingo free?

Yes, StudyLingo is free to use.

How do I earn points?

By answering quiz questions correctly, reading study notes and logging in on consecutive days. Points are never given for viewing or clicking ads.

Do points have any cash value?

No. Points are for learning, badges and the leaderboard. They cannot be exchanged for money.

Why do I need to sign in?

So your points, streak and progress are saved safely to your own account. Questions are only visible after sign-in.

Will others see my real name?

No. You choose a nickname and an avatar, and only those are shown on leaderboards.

Who runs StudyLingo?

StudyLingo was founded by Shahjad Ali, a developer and tech enthusiast. See About Us and Contact Us in the footer.
